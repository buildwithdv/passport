01About Passport

This policy explains how Passport handles information through its Chrome extension and supporting service. Passport helps users share authorized access to supported browser-based AI tools with trusted recipients by transferring encrypted browser-session data.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Passport publisher at no_email_shared@proton.me. Independent websites opened through Passport have their own privacy policies and account-sharing rules.

02Information we handle

InformationWhat it includes and where it is used
Account informationYour chosen username, recovery email, account identifier and creation information are processed by the service. Recovery email addresses are not verified.
Device & authentication dataBrowser-profile credentials, device identifiers and encryption keys support account ownership and delivery to registered receiving devices. Private receiving keys are stored in the browser profile; public keys and device registration information are sent to the service.
Shared website sessionsCookies, authentication tokens, localStorage and sessionStorage from websites selected for sharing. Session content is encrypted on the sending device; the service relays the encrypted bundle to the chosen recipient.
Website & share metadataOpen-tab titles and URLs are read locally to populate the website selector. Selected origins, sender and recipient identifiers, access duration, timestamps and share status are used to deliver and manage shares.
Payment & licence recordsPurchase identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, licence records and activation details support paid features. Dodo Payments processes checkout information; Passport does not intentionally collect raw payment-card numbers.
Support & operational dataInformation you send to support. Infrastructure providers may also process connection metadata such as IP addresses, request timestamps and error details to deliver and protect the service.
Session data can be sensitive. Website storage can contain conversations, files, personal information or other sensitive content in addition to sign-in tokens. Passport does not classify every storage value. Avoid sharing financial, health, government, identity or administrator accounts.

03How we use information

  • Create and maintain your Passport identity and registered devices.
  • Find recipients by username and deliver the session access you choose to share.
  • Display sent and received shares, access windows and status.
  • Check expiry or revocation and attempt cleanup on receiving devices.
  • Apply free-share allowances, process purchases and verify licence entitlements.
  • Respond to support requests, troubleshoot failures and prevent abuse.

Passport does not use shared website data to train AI models, create advertising profiles or determine creditworthiness. The extension does not intentionally scrape AI conversations or page content; however, such content may be present in the website storage included in a share.

04Who receives information

The recipient you choose

Your chosen recipient receives access to the encrypted session bundle, which their registered receiving device decrypts. A shared session may let them view conversations, files, history, account settings or other information available through the original website. They also see the share information needed to identify and open it.

Service providers

Cloudflare Workers and D1 provide Passport’s backend hosting and database infrastructure. Dodo Payments processes checkout and related purchase or licence events. Support email is handled through the email service used for our contact address. Providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies, and information may be processed outside your country.

06Encryption and its limits

Passport encrypts session bundles on the sender’s device using AES-GCM and wraps the encryption key for registered receiving devices using RSA-OAEP. Connections to the Passport service use HTTPS. The service receives encrypted session content along with the metadata needed to deliver and manage the share.

Decrypted session data is restored into the recipient’s browser so the website can use it. Private keys and browser-profile credentials remain sensitive local data. Encryption cannot protect against a compromised device, malicious recipient or information copied after access is opened.

Expiry and revocation depend on the receiving extension being enabled, connected and allowed to clean up. They do not guarantee that copied credentials become unusable. Use the original website’s session controls when you need to revoke access at the source.

Opening a share can replace existing sign-in and saved website data. Use a separate Chrome profile where appropriate. Never send cookies, session tokens, private keys or payment details to support.

07How long information remains

Browser-profile data remains until it is removed by Passport, you clear the extension’s storage, or you remove the profile or extension. Removing Passport does not by itself erase server records or guarantee removal of data restored into another website.

The backend is configured to clear encrypted share payloads after expiry or revocation during scheduled hourly maintenance. Cleanup is not instantaneous and can be delayed by service interruptions. Share metadata is retained separately for account and sharing management.

Account, device, share and purchase records remain while needed to operate the service, maintain entitlements, prevent abuse, handle disputes or meet applicable obligations. These records currently do not all have an automatic deletion period. Contact us to request deletion and ask about the retention applicable to your records. Support messages, infrastructure logs and backups may follow separate provider or operational retention schedules.

08Your choices and requests

  • Choose whether to share, which website to share, the recipient and the access window.
  • End received access or revoke outgoing shares through Passport, subject to the cleanup limits above.
  • Review and revoke website permissions through Chrome’s extension settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable law and identity verification.

Include your Passport username and describe your request. Do not email credentials or session data. A recovery email alone is not proof of ownership because recovery addresses are unverified. We may request additional verification before changing or deleting account records.

Clearing browser storage or changing profiles may remove the local credentials used to identify you. It does not automatically transfer your account or single-profile licence activation to another installation.

09Limited use of user data

Passport’s use of information received through Chrome extension APIs is limited to providing and improving its disclosed access-sharing features, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell user data, use or transfer it for personalized advertising, or use it for lending or creditworthiness decisions. Transfers are limited to delivering the requested features, necessary service providers, security, legal obligations or permitted ownership changes. Human access to user data is limited to the exceptions allowed by that policy, such as your explicit consent for a specific support request, necessary security investigations or legal obligations.

This privacy page includes no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, external fonts or tracking cookies. Your hosting provider may still process ordinary connection and request information when serving it.
